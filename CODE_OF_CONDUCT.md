# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We want npm-safe to be a welcoming, technically rigorous project. Participants
are expected to communicate with respect, discuss ideas rather than people,
accept constructive feedback, and help keep security reports responsible and
evidence-based.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, deliberate disruption, publishing another person's private
information, or disclosing an unpatched vulnerability without reasonable
coordination.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests,
security communications, and other spaces where someone represents the
project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **1031402408@qq.com**. Maintainers may edit
or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community.

Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as
practical. Retaliation against a reporter is not acceptable.
